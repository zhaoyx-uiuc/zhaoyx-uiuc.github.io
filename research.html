Research

Research Interests

  • UAS-based infrastructure inspection and optimized path planning
  • Uncertainty-guided photogrammetry and 3D reconstruction of bridges
  • Remote sensing and uncertainty characterization
  • Human sensing, assistive navigation, and digital twins
  • Computer vision for structural damage detection

Research Themes

AUTOMATED INFRASTRUCTURE INSPECTION

Optimizing UAS inspection and reconstruction

My work develops optimization frameworks for UAS-based infrastructure inspection path planning. Publications address automated structural inspection and uncertainty-guided, five-degree-of-freedom path planning for photogrammetric 3D reconstruction of bridges.

Related work: Automation in Construction (2026, 2024); ASCE International Conference on Computing in Civil Engineering (2023).

HUMAN–MACHINE INTERACTION

Assistive navigation and digital inspection platforms

Collaborative work explores a human-in-the-loop assistive navigation platform for UAS visual inspection, human sensing platforms for digitizing inspections of critical infrastructure, and tangible digital twins that convey structural stiffness through hand gestures in virtual reality.

Related work: Sensors (2026); ASEE Annual Conference & Exposition (2024); Structures Congress (2023).

REMOTE SENSING

UAS-based soil moisture sensing

Collaborative research investigates UAV-based soil moisture measurements across diverse land covers, including validation and Bayesian uncertainty characterization. Related work assesses high-resolution retrievals from a UAS-mounted L-band radiometer across vegetation gradients.

Related work: IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (2025); IGARSS (2025).

MATERIALS & STRUCTURES

Experimental performance and visual damage detection

Earlier work examines the effects of fly ash and crumb rubber on ECC mechanical properties and permeability, timber beams strengthened with engineered bamboo laminates, and the influence of moisture content on Moso bamboo. Building façade studies explore convolutional neural networks for crack detection and intelligent damage identification.

Related work: Journal of Building Engineering (2023, 2022); Construction and Building Materials (2022); Building Structure; Construction Technology.
