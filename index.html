Yuxiang Zhao

PhD Candidate in Civil Engineering

Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

My research focuses on unmanned aerial systems (UAS) for infrastructure inspection, optimized flight path planning, and photogrammetric 3D reconstruction. My work also spans remote sensing, human-centered inspection technologies, and the experimental behavior of construction materials and structures.

I joined the PhD program in Civil Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in August 2022. I earned a Master of Science in Engineering in Civil Engineering from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, in April 2020 (August 2019–April 2020), and a Bachelor of Engineering in Civil Engineering from Tongji University in June 2019 (September 2015–June 2019). Before starting my PhD, I worked as a scientific project researcher at the Shanghai Research Institute of Building Sciences.
